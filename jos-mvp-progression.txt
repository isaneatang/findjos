PRODUCT DIRECTION
-----------------
Build a broad Jos business discovery platform, but launch it within one dense
geographic area. Do not position the product as food-only. The directory may
include food, technology, repairs, fashion, hotels, gyms, furniture, cars,
rentals, and other services from the beginning, while the physical coverage
starts small and expands gradually.

The core promise:

    Find businesses, products, and services near you in Jos.

The strongest early advantage is not complicated software. It is a useful,
accurate, locally collected dataset with landmarks, contact details, photos,
categories, services, and searchable product keywords.


GEOGRAPHIC LAUNCH STRATEGY
--------------------------
Start with one compact, high-activity commercial area instead of all of Jos.
A possible starting zone is Terminus / Ahmadu Bello Way and nearby technology,
food, shopping, repair, and service businesses.

The sequence is:

1. Make one dense area useful.
2. Add a second nearby commercial area.
3. Expand to University of Jos and student-heavy areas.
4. Expand to Rayfield, Bukuru, Farin Gada, Angwan Rukuba, and other areas.
5. Expand outside Jos only after the local model works.

Each area should have enough useful listings before moving to the next one.


LISTING LIFECYCLE
-----------------
Listings have three practical states:

1. UNCLAIMED
   Collected and published by the administrator. The business owner does not
   need an account. It contains basic information, a source, collection date,
   and verification status.

2. CLAIMED
   The owner has confirmed the listing and can edit approved fields, such as
   phone number, hours, services, photos, and description.

3. VERIFIED / FEATURED
   The administrator has reviewed the business or approved a paid enhancement.
   It may receive a verified checkmark, richer profile, more photos, product
   entries, performance reports, and priority placement.

The administrator must be able to change a listing's status, approve edits,
mark businesses as closed, hide listings, and remove duplicates.


BUSINESS CATEGORIES
-------------------
The initial directory should support multiple categories, including:

- Food and restaurants
- Mobile phones, laptops, and electronics
- Phone and computer repair
- Fashion, tailors, and clothing
- Salons and barbers
- Printing and graphics
- Furniture
- Car dealers and car services
- Gyms
- Hotels and short-let apartments
- Rentals
- Supermarkets and provision stores

Technology businesses are a strong early outreach group because they often
already advertise through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, banners, or photos.
They may be more receptive to claiming a listing and later managing products.


DEMO PHASE
----------
The demo proves the concept before building a full platform. It should use
approximately 30 to 50 manually entered businesses from one area and at least
six categories.

Demo capabilities:

- Search by business name, category, service, or product keyword
- Browse by category
- Filter by area or landmark
- Open a business detail page
- See photos, services, hours, landmark, and contact details
- Call a business
- Start a WhatsApp conversation
- Open an optional external map link
- Add and edit listings through a simple administrator-only process

Demo exclusions:

- Consumer accounts
- Business owner accounts
- Payments
- In-app messaging
- Ordering or delivery
- Full live inventory
- Complex ratings and reviews

The demo can use manual records and a simple database. It does not need to
solve every operational problem yet.


MVP PHASE
---------
The MVP turns the demo into a real, maintainable product. Target approximately
100 to 300 listings across the first few areas.

Public product:

- Fast mobile-first search and browse
- Multiple categories and keywords
- Business detail pages
- WhatsApp and phone actions
- Landmark-based location information
- Optional map link
- Listing status and verification indicators
- Report incorrect information

Business-owner product:

- Claim a listing
- Secure owner login
- Submit edits for approval
- Upload compressed photos
- Update hours, services, descriptions, and contacts
- Add a small number of products or menu items
- View basic views, search appearances, calls, and WhatsApp taps

Administrator product:

- Secure administrator login
- Dashboard summary
- Create custom listings
- Edit any listing
- Approve, reject, hide, archive, or restore listings
- Approve or reject owner claims
- Add or remove the verified checkmark
- Manage categories, tags, areas, landmarks, and services
- Review reported errors
- Merge duplicate businesses
- Mark businesses as closed or needing re-verification
- Import listings from a spreadsheet where practical
- View basic listing and contact analytics

Still exclude from the first MVP:

- Consumer accounts
- Native mobile applications
- In-app chat
- Payments and commissions
- Delivery
- Complex review systems
- Real-time inventory for every business


ADMINISTRATOR DASHBOARD PRINCIPLES
----------------------------------
The administrator dashboard is essential because the initial supply is
manually collected and data quality is the platform's main asset.

Every listing should have administrative metadata that users do not necessarily
see:

- Listing status: draft, published, hidden, archived, or closed
- Ownership status: unclaimed or claimed
- Verification status and verification date
- Source of the data
- Last reviewed date
- Next review reminder, if needed
- Created by and last updated by
- Duplicate or report notes

Use role-based permissions from the beginning. At minimum, distinguish between
an administrator and a business owner. Do not expose administrator controls in
the public application.


RECOMMENDED TECHNICAL FOUNDATION
--------------------------------
- Frontend and backend: Next.js with TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- Database, authentication, and file storage: Supabase
- Hosting and deployment previews: Vercel
- Images: compressed WebP or similarly efficient formats
- Search initially: database text search; add a dedicated search engine only
  when the dataset or search quality requires it
- Maps initially: landmark text and external map links; defer heavy embedded
  maps until they provide clear value
- Communication: phone links and WhatsApp links instead of in-app chat

Keep the first architecture cheap and easy to replace. Avoid paid search,
geocoding, messaging, and infrastructure services until real usage justifies
them.


VERCEL WORKFLOW
---------------
Vercel should be used early, not only after the product is finished. It lets
the developer see the real mobile web experience and share a working preview.

Basic workflow:

1. Create the Next.js project locally.
2. Put the project in a GitHub repository.
3. Import the repository into Vercel.
4. Configure environment variables for Supabase.
5. Deploy the main branch for a persistent demo URL.
6. Use preview deployments for changes before merging them.
7. Test every preview on a cheap Android phone and a slow connection.

The free tiers are appropriate for the demo and early MVP, but usage limits
must be monitored. Control costs by compressing images, avoiding unnecessary
map tiles, keeping database queries simple, and not storing large originals.
Purchase a domain only when the public demo is stable enough to share widely.


BUILD ORDER
-----------
1. Confirm the first launch area and collect a starter spreadsheet.
2. Create the Next.js application and deploy a blank shell to Vercel.
3. Set up Supabase and define listings, categories, areas, users, and reports.
4. Build the public browse, search, and listing-detail pages.
5. Build the administrator login and listing-management dashboard.
6. Import 30 to 50 real businesses and test the demo on mobile.
7. Add WhatsApp, call, and external map actions.
8. Add listing reports, statuses, and verification controls.
9. Add owner claiming and editing after the public workflow is useful.
10. Add analytics and paid visibility only after real interaction data exists.


SUCCESS CHECKS
--------------
The demo is ready when:

- A user can find a relevant business in under a minute.
- Search works across several categories and local keywords.
- A user can contact a business without creating an account.
- The administrator can create, edit, verify, hide, and restore a listing.
- Listing data can be corrected without editing source code.
- The site works on a low-end mobile device.
- The application is deployed through Vercel and can be shared by URL.

The MVP is ready for wider expansion when:

- The initial area has meaningful coverage.
- Users return and use the contact actions.
- Businesses ask to be added or claim listings.
- Listing updates can be managed without becoming overwhelming.
- Hosting and storage usage are understood.


CORE REMINDER
-------------
Do not build the entire marketplace before proving the local discovery loop.
The first loop is:

    Search -> Find a useful listing -> Contact the business -> Return later

The code should support that loop, while the administrator dashboard protects
the accuracy and growth of the business dataset.


ACCOUNT AND ACCESS MODEL
------------------------
The product has three account roles, but only two should be required for the
first usable workflow.

1. VIEWER / CUSTOMER
   Anyone can browse, search, filter, and open listings without registering or
   signing in. This is important because asking a new visitor to create an
   account before checking services adds unnecessary friction.

   A viewer account is optional. It can later support saved businesses,
   saved searches, report history, personalised recommendations, or alerts.
   For the first MVP, anonymous browsing and contact actions are sufficient.

2. BUSINESS OWNER / SELLER
   A seller creates an account or accepts an invitation after an administrator
   creates the initial listing. The seller can claim one or more businesses,
   submit profile changes, manage approved photos and services, and optionally
   add products. Seller edits should enter a pending-review state before they
   become public.

3. ADMINISTRATOR
   An administrator has a protected dashboard and full control over listings,
   users, categories, claims, reports, verification, and publication status.
   Administrator accounts must never be created through an open public signup
   form. Create the initial administrator manually and add other administrators
   only from the protected dashboard.

PUBLIC ACCESS RULE
------------------
No registration is required to:

- Open the home page
- Search for businesses, products, or services
- Browse categories and areas
- Open a business profile
- Call a business
- Start a WhatsApp conversation
- Open a map link
- Report incorrect information, subject to basic abuse protection

Registration is required only for seller actions and optional viewer features.


AUTHENTICATION FLOW ON PAPER
----------------------------
Anonymous viewer:

    Visit site -> Search or browse -> Open listing -> Contact business

Seller claiming an existing listing:

    Find listing -> Select Claim this business -> Sign in or create seller
    account -> Submit proof/contact details -> Administrator reviews -> Claim
    approved -> Seller can edit approved fields

Seller creating a new business:

    Sign in as seller -> Submit business -> Administrator reviews -> Listing
    published or returned for correction

Administrator:

    Open protected admin URL -> Sign in -> Review dashboard -> Create/edit/
    approve/hide/verify listings and process claims or reports

The first administrator can provision seller accounts and manually link a
seller to a listing while the claim workflow is still being developed.


INITIAL DEMO ACCOUNT SETUP
--------------------------
Use clearly fake accounts and dummy businesses during development:

- One administrator account for the project owner
- Two or three seller accounts representing different business types
- Anonymous viewer mode as the default public experience
- Optional test viewer account only if saved features are implemented

Never commit passwords, service-role keys, or authentication secrets to the
repository. Store them in local environment variables and Vercel environment
variables.


CURRENT DEMO DECISIONS
----------------------
- Product name: FindJos
- First geographic area: Terminus, Jos
- Initial data: clearly labelled fictional/mock businesses for the demo
- Seller authentication: email and password
- Viewer access: anonymous browsing; viewer registration is optional and not
  required before searching
- Administrator authentication: protected email/password account with an
  administrator role

Do not scrape or copy Google Maps business data for the demo. Use fictional
businesses with invented names, contacts, images, and addresses. Real map data
can be added later through an approved maps API or manually verified field
research, subject to the provider's terms. External map links should not imply
that a fictional business is real.
